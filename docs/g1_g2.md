# Stage 3 - G1/G2 selection

From the WaterFP of the ligand heavy atoms:

- **G1** (anti-bulk): the most negative FP atom, and a partner among the next most negative
  that is not bonded to it, the farthest one while FP values (rounded) stay within 0.2.
- **G2** (bulk): the least negative FP atom, and the next least negative one at least three
  bonds away.

These are the rules of WaterFP's `fp_driven_atom_selection.ipynb` (commit `6a5aef7`),
vendored in `tests/reference/` and checked against `select_g1_g2` on hundreds of random ligands.

The monitor already selects on its production FP, once the selection is settled within the FP
errors. By hand:

```bash
oneopes select-block --tpr prod.tpr --run blocks.nc --outdir sel [--block N]     # production as block N's check found it
oneopes select --tpr prod.tpr --xtc prod.xtc --outdir sel --first-frame A --last-frame B   # any window
```

The topology must have bonds (a `.tpr`). Writes `run_metadata.json`, `rdf.csv` (`atom,r_nm,n_r`),
`fingerprints.csv` (`atom,fp`) and `g1_g2.yaml`:

```yaml
G1: [{name: <atom>, serial: <n>}, {name: <atom>, serial: <n>}]
G2: [{name: <atom>, serial: <n>}, {name: <atom>, serial: <n>}]
```

`serial` is the 1-based topology number, as GROMACS and PLUMED count atoms.
